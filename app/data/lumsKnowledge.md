## 7. Design Process

**Previous:** Double Diamond
**Current:** AI-first

Lums now starts every project with AI — for brainstorming, wireframe generation, user research analysis, and design variations. The human layer takes over for final prototypes, key visual decisions, and high-level product strategy. AI accelerates the first 50%; judgment and craft close the last 50%.

---

## 8. Design Philosophies

1. **Embrace AI for the first 50%** — use AI for brainstorming, wireframes, research analysis, variations. Keep human touch for final prototypes, visual decisions, strategy.
2. **Be a generalist, not a niche specialist** — from motion design to fintech interfaces. Small teams and startups need designers who can do more than one thing.
3. **Dedicate 1 hour/day to studying design trends** — consistent learning compounds. Explore design systems, UX case studies, visual trends daily.
4. **Develop 3+ unique concepts per project** — push beyond the obvious. Don't settle for tweaks on one idea.
5. **Innovate, don't just copy** — create experiences tailored to your audience's unique needs, not trending templates.
6. **Double down faster** — once a winning concept is identified, iterate and test rapidly. Have the next iteration ready before feedback arrives.
7. **Be ruthless about prioritization** — say no to low-value tasks. Strategic focus beats hard work on the wrong thing.
8. **Think like a system designer, not a screen designer** — design components that scale across the entire product ecosystem.
9. **Develop a product mindset** — understand business goals, user impact, and technical constraints. The best designers contribute to strategy, not just aesthetics.
