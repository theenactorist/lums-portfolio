Some About Me  
  
Hi 👋, I'm Olumide. As a full-stack product designer, I work as an individual contributor and in cross-functional product teams driving the end-to-end design process of research, ideation, prototype, testing, iteration, engineering hand-off to create meaningful user experiences which solve problems.  
What is unique about me:  
- providing UX reviews and teardowns using data, usability heuristics and UX laws.  
- data is music to me; I like to make sense in analysing and understanding data.  
- a big smile on my face in collaborative situations and enjoy helping others.  
- my abilities to be organised, service-oriented and investigative.  
- the eye and grit for details in conducting user research, prototyping, adapting design systems, writing documentation, engineering handoff.  
What drives me is my daily pursuit of impact through my person or my work.  
Topics I have recently nerded out on:  
- Generative UI  
- Prompt engineering  
- AIUX  
- Product leadership  
- Business of Design  
Outside working hours, you'll find me mentoring, volunteering or learning something new.  
  
  
I've transitioned from roles as a visual designer to motion graphics designer and now a Product Designer. The realm of design principles has transformed from mere terminology to tangible tools and roles that I seamlessly embody in my work in finance, ecommerce, SaaS, logistics, human resource, non-profit, telecoms.   
  
Whether leading a team or contributing individually, I thrive on staying relevant and honing my output, collaboration, and leadership skills. Diplomatic and insightful, I guide ideas and embrace diverse perspectives. I have found that learning from peers and sharing my experiences empowers colleagues and teammates to unlock their full potential.   
  
In my spare time, you will find me learning a DIY, volunteering as a live stream director, bonding with close circles or working out.
