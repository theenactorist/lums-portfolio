**Olumide’s Playbook**  
A guide for productive collaboration with me  
  
**Introduction**  
Holla, this doc aims to clarify my working style so we can collaborate more effectively. By sharing my idiosyncrasies and guiding principles, I hope to help you anticipate challenges and understand who I am. **Please note that this guide is not static but will adapt as my leadership evolves so challenge me if you observe any deviation. **  
  
**Summary**  
1. Olumide places high importance on continuous learning and self-development. He appreciates proactively seeking knowledge to improve proficiency on the job.  
2. He firmly believes in a “can do” attitude and enjoys pushing the boundaries and testing the limits. Embracing experimentation is a practice he keeps.  
3. Success to Olumide means growth of innovation/initiatives among teammates, executing well and the identification and resolution of bottlenecks.  
4. He communicates judiciously, especially in response to well-thought-out ideas and problems.   
5. Olumide may resort to micro-management when a teammate fails to execute repeatedly. His frequent questioning could indicate insufficient clarity in an execution.  
6. Frequently missing deadlines and communication lapses (skipping, no response, not looking out for) put a cog in the wheel.  
7. Olumide’s strengths include his ability to make deductions and pursue an objective. He values vulnerability and encourages problem-solving.  
8. Areas requiring growth with him include impatience with errors and a tendency to take on problem-solving tasks himself rather than delegating especially after a period of inefficiency.  
  
**Major**  
My preference is to be efficient, strategy-focused and lead instead of meddling in operations. I get excitement from bringing an idea to life or an initiative to experimentation. I thrive in teams where each member knows what is at stake and is equally motivated to achieve the common objective. I do better with a 300 of Gideon than a 180,000 of Rehoboam – where each member's contribution is pivotal and indispensable for the team's overall success.
