## Evidence rules

- Every image must prove the argument made immediately before it.
- Use annotations to identify the relevant difference instead of relying on a zoomed-out board.
- Prefer failed-versus-fixed comparisons for colour, camera, material, and meaning.
- Keep original source screenshots intact except for cropping, redaction, and annotation needed for readability.
- Do not extract the outdated illustration count, rollout date, or geographic details from recognition screenshots into public prose without separate confirmation.
- Do not use article wording as Olumide's portfolio copy. The artefacts are evidence; the narrative must be rewritten independently.
