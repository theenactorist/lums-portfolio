# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, design directors, senior product-design peers, and potential collaborators reviewing Olumide Olusesi's portfolio. They need to assess role fit quickly, then understand the judgment, craft, ownership, and systems thinking behind selected work.

## Product Purpose

Present Olumide as a senior product designer who combines foundational design practice, behavioural science, AI fluency, and technical execution. The portfolio should make complex work easy to scan without flattening the decisions, constraints, collaboration, and evidence that demonstrate seniority.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and expressive. The experience should feel assured and selective, with enough narrative tension and visual evidence to make the work memorable. The writing should remain warm, direct, and easy to understand.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated portfolio templates and SaaS landing-page tropes.
- Decorative interfaces that obscure the work or repeat the same card treatment throughout the page.
- Process theatre, inflated leadership language, vague superlatives, and unsupported claims.
- Copied language, personal branding, client lists, or proprietary assets from reference portfolios.
- Dense case studies that force readers to reconstruct the problem, ownership, or outcome.

## Design Principles

- Treat the portfolio as a product and the recruiter as its primary user.
- Lead with the consequence, decision, or outcome, then support it with evidence.
- Show craft through real artefacts, product screens, system files, and implementation details.
- Preserve factual ownership and make team contributions clear.
- Use references as structural guidance while keeping Olumide's work, voice, and brand distinct.
- Make every section earn its place through a meaningful decision, constraint, result, or learning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain readable contrast, semantic heading order, keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives, accessible sticky navigation, and responsive layouts that do not depend on hover.
