# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Hiring managers, design directors, and potential clients. They are reviewing this portfolio to evaluate the designer's maturity, strategic thinking, and technical capability in high-stakes environments.

## Product Purpose
To showcase the work of an AI-savvy designer who is deeply rooted in foundational UX principles and strategic thinking. It serves as undeniable proof of exceptional craft, structured product design, and modern technical execution.

## Brand Personality
Minimal, technical, and modern (inspired by tools like Linear). The tone should feel sharp, precise, and highly intentional, conveying quiet confidence.

## Anti-references
"AI slop" – The site should absolutely avoid looking like thoughtlessly generated AI templates, generic SaaS tropes, or chaotic overstuffed layouts. It must feel deliberately crafted, constrained, and human-directed.

## Design Principles
- **Show, don't just tell:** Demonstrate AI savviness and technical capability through flawless execution and interaction, rather than just relying on buzzwords.
- **Foundational UX first:** Ensure the core structure, typography, and information architecture are perfect before adding technical flair or motion.
- **Precision over noise:** Keep the design minimal and technical. Every element on the screen must earn its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No strict compliance requirements, but standard best practices for contrast and readability should be maintained to ensure a high-quality baseline.
